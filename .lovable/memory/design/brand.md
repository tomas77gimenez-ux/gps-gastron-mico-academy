## Colors
- Background: pure black (oklch 0.10)
- Card/surfaces: dark gray (oklch 0.14-0.22)
- Primary/accent: orange (oklch 0.70 0.18 45)
- Text: white/light gray (oklch 0.95)
- Muted text: medium gray (oklch 0.55)
- Borders: white at 8% opacity

## Typography
- Display/headings: Space Grotesk (400-700)
- Body: Inter (300-900)
- Logo: "GPS Gastronômico" with gradient-brand class
- Tagline: "Gestión · Procesos · Sustentabilidad"

## Effects
- glow-orange: box-shadow with primary color glow
- text-gradient-brand: orange gradient text
- Cards hover: border-primary/30 + subtle orange shadow
